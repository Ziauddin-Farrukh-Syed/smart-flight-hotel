HOTEL INFORMATION

Hotel ratings are commonly expressed using a scale from 1 to 5 stars, although rating systems can vary by country and provider.

A higher rating generally indicates a higher level of facilities and services, but the rating alone does not guarantee that a particular amenity is available.

Check-in and check-out times vary by hotel.

Some hotels require a security deposit or temporary card authorization.

Breakfast may be included or excluded depending on the selected rate.

Some hotel rates are refundable and others are non-refundable.

The travel agent should use provider data to determine the actual hotel price, cancellation policy, breakfast inclusion and other booking conditions.
