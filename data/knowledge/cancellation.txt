CANCELLATION AND REFUND INFORMATION

Cancellation and refund conditions depend on the airline, hotel, fare and booking conditions.

Some flight fares are refundable while others may be non-refundable.

Some fares allow changes for a fee while others may allow changes without a change fee but require payment of any fare difference.

Hotel cancellation policies vary by property and rate.

A refundable hotel rate may have a cancellation deadline.

A non-refundable hotel rate may not provide a refund after booking.

The travel agent must display the cancellation conditions provided by the booking provider.

The agent must not claim that a booking is refundable unless the provider data confirms it.
