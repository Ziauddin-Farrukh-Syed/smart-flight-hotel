CABIN CLASSES

Economy class is generally the lowest standard cabin class and normally provides the lowest fare.

Premium Economy generally provides more space and additional services compared with standard economy.

Business class generally provides a higher level of comfort and service. Depending on the airline and aircraft, this may include wider seats, priority services, lounge access and lie-flat seats.

First class is generally the highest cabin class offered by an airline and may provide additional privacy, service and amenities.

The exact services included in each cabin class vary by airline, route, aircraft and fare type.

The travel agent should not assume that a particular service is included unless the provider data confirms it.
