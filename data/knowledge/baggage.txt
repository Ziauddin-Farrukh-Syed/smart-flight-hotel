BAGGAGE INFORMATION

Baggage allowances depend on the airline, fare type, route and cabin class.

Economy passengers may have a smaller checked baggage allowance than business class passengers.

Business class passengers commonly receive a larger checked baggage allowance, but the exact allowance must always be confirmed with the airline operating the flight.

Cabin baggage restrictions also vary by airline.

Passengers should check the baggage allowance associated with the exact fare before booking.

Additional baggage may incur additional charges.

For connecting flights, baggage rules may vary depending on whether the flights are on the same ticket and which airlines operate the journey.
